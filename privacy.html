Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Namo Auto Publisher is a tool designed to help authorized users publish their own short-form videos through automated workflows.

1. Information We Process

When you authorize the application, it may process account identifiers, granted permissions, access tokens, uploaded videos, captions, and publishing status information.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate authorized users, upload their videos, publish content they request, and troubleshoot publishing errors.

3. Third-Party Services

The application may use Telegram for receiving content, n8n for workflow automation, Supabase for video storage, and TikTok's authorized APIs for publishing.

4. Storage and Security

We use access controls to protect application credentials and restrict access to workflow administration. Videos stored using public links may be accessible to anyone with those links.

5. Your Choices

You may revoke the application's TikTok access through your TikTok account settings. You may also contact the application operator to request information about retained data or its deletion.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.
